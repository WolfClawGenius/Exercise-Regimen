Full-Body Dynamic Warmup Library

Prepare your tendons, lubricate joint capsules, and safely elevate core temperature with 18 ACE-curated mobility drills designed for longevity.

Browse All 18 Drills Jump to Active Circuit
Shoulder Mobility

1. Arm Circles (Small to Large)

Gradually enlarge shoulder circles from small rotations to sweeping overhead crosses without arching the lower back.

Target: Deltoids / Cuff 10 reps each dir
Scapular Rhythm

2. Wall Slides (W to Y)

Keep forearms and wrists sliding up a flat wall from a 'W' position into a full overhead 'Y' to prime the mid-traps.

Target: Scapula / Mid-Traps 10 slow reps
Thoracic Opener

3. Dynamic Chest Expander Hugs

Alternate wrapping arms across your chest in a self-hug, then opening wide to retract scapulae and stretch pecs.

Target: Chest / Upper Back 12 reps alternating
Forearm & Wrist

4. Wrist Circles & Figure-8s

Interlace fingers and roll wrists in continuous infinity-loop patterns to prime joints for counter or wall push-ups[cite: 1, 2].

Target: Wrists / Flexors 30 seconds
Shoulder Elevation

5. Dynamic Shoulder Rolls

Lift shoulders smoothly up toward the ears, glide them down the back ribcage, and release accumulated upper back tension.

Target: Trapezius / Neck 10 rolls each dir
Cervical Mobility

6. Cervical Tilts & Half-Rolls

Drop chin to chest and roll ear to shoulder smoothly. Avoid full backward head rolls to safeguard the cervical spine.

Target: Neck / Scalenes 6 rolls each side
Spinal Articulation

7. Standing Cat-Cow

Rest hands on upper thighs; flex the spine outward like a cat, then extend forward, drawing the sternum forward.

Target: Spinal Column 8 breath cycles
Rotational Core

8. Torso Rotations with Pivot

Keep knees softly unlocked and pivot the trailing heel as your arms sweep comfortably across the body.

Target: Obliques / T-Spine 15 reps alternating
Posterior Chain

9. Standing Hip Hinge (Good Mornings)

Place hands at temples, soft bend in knees, push the hips backward until hamstrings load, then return erect.

Target: Glutes / Hamstrings 10 controlled reps
Lateral Core

10. Standing Side Bend Reach

Slide one hand down the outer thigh while sweeping the opposite arm overhead, maintaining an upright, neutral pelvis.

Target: Quadratus Lumborum 8 reps per side
Pelvic Alignment

11. Pelvic Circles (Hula Hips)

With hands on hips, trace wide circular patterns with the pelvis to lubricate lower lumbar joints and sacroiliac region.

Target: Pelvis / Sacroiliac 8 circles each way
Diagonal Core

12. Standing Dynamic Woodchopper

Clasp hands and sweep diagonally from high shoulder down to opposing hip, pivoting feet to protect the knee.

Target: Anterior Sling / Abs 10 reps per side
Hip Extension

13. Supported Leg Swings (Front/Back)

Lightly touch a wall for balance and swing one leg forward and backward like a pendulum to mobilize hip flexors.

Target: Hip Flexors / Glutes 12 swings each leg
Groin & Adductors

14. Lateral Leg Swings (Side-to-Side)

Facing a wall, swing your leg smoothly across the midline and out to the side to warm inner and outer hip musculature.

Target: Adductor / Abductor 10 swings each leg
Hip Capsule

15. Gate Openers (Hip Openers)

Lift knee to 90 degrees, trace an arc outward to open the hip capsule, and step down smoothly before alternating.

Target: Hip Capsule 8 reps per side
Lower Leg Prep

16. Heel-to-Toe Rockers

Stand upright and roll smoothly from your heels (lifting toes) up onto the balls of your feet to warm calves and shins.

Target: Calves / Tibialis 15 smooth rocks
Knee Lubrication

17. Shallow Air Squats (Quarter Depth)

Keep feet grounded and track knees over toes. Dip 4–6 inches to pump synovial fluid to the patella without loading joints[cite: 2].

Target: Quadriceps / Knees 10 reps
Ankle Capsule

18. Ankle Circles & Alphabets

Lift one foot off the floor and trace full rotational circles or letters A–M with your big toe to loosen the ankle joint.

Target: Ankle Complex 10 circles each way
