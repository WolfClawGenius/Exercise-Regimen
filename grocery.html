Mini-Fridge Grocery List

Optimized strictly for a 3.2 cu. ft. compact refrigerator footprint and dry storage.

Pantry Items (Zero Fridge Space Needed)

  • Canned Goods: 2 cans black beans, 2 cans chickpeas, 2 cans lentil soup
  • Canned Seafood: 3–4 cans wild pink salmon, sardines, or light tuna
  • Grains & Wraps: Rolled oats (plain), high-fiber whole-wheat tortillas, 2–3 pouches 90-sec brown rice
  • Fats & Nuts: Extra virgin olive oil, raw walnuts or almonds, chia seeds or ground flaxseed, natural peanut butter
  • Counter Produce: 3–4 avocados, 1 bag apples, 1 container cherry tomatoes

Mini-Fridge Placement (3.2 cu. ft.)

Compartment Item Storage Strategy
Top Shelf Plain Greek yogurt (32 oz) Keep upright in corner
Top Shelf Pre-cooked hard-boiled eggs (6 pk) Lay flat next to yogurt
Bottom Shelf Rotisserie chicken (deboned) Discard plastic dome; pack meat into 1-quart zip bag
Bottom Shelf Baby spinach (8–10 oz bag) Flexible bag sits alongside chicken
Door Bins Mustard, hot sauce, salsa, block cheese Utilize side door shelves and butter keeper
Freezer Compartment Steam-in-bag frozen broccoli / green beans Stack 2 bags flat inside small upper freezer
