Exercise Technique Reference

Detailed movement execution, setup instructions, and safety checkpoints for every drill in the protocol.

Chair Sit-to-Stands (Power Squat)

The quadriceps and glutes represent the largest muscle mass in the body. Elevating pacing here triggers high metabolic output without overloading patellar tendons.

  • Sit near the front lip of a sturdy chair with feet flat and shoulder-width apart.
  • Drive directly through the mid-foot and heels to stand erect, contracting glutes fully at lockout.
  • Hinge backwards at the hips and lower under control until you lightly graze the chair before pressing up again.

Low-Impact Step Jacks

A zero-impact alternative to standard jumping jacks that keeps oxygen demand elevated without joint stress through the lower ankles and knees.

  • Start standing upright with feet side-by-side and hands relaxed at your sides.
  • Step the right foot out wide while sweeping both arms overhead simultaneously.
  • Return to center smoothly, then immediately alternate to step out wide with the left foot.

Incline Wall / Counter Push-Ups

Preserves upper-body push strength across the pectorals and triceps while easing wrist extension angles and relieving lumbar compression.

  • Place palms flat against a counter or wall surface, slightly wider than shoulder width.
  • Descend slowly until the chest nears the contact surface while holding a rigid abdominal plank.
  • Drive away firmly without abruptly hyperextending or locking the elbows.

Standing March with Core Rotation

Challenges single-leg stability, trains diagonal abdominal cross-chains, and sustains target heart rates without ground-based floor exercises.

  • Stand tall with hands held comfortably at chest height or fingertips behind ears.
  • Lift one knee smoothly toward belt height while subtly turning the opposing shoulder toward it.
  • Step down smoothly and cycle to the opposite side in an alternating cadence.
