15-Day Metabolic Meal Plan

Structured in two phases to prevent spoilage in a 3.2 cu. ft. fridge while targeting blood sugar and lipids.

Estimated macros: Calories and macros are rounded estimates per meal using standard portions, USDA FoodData Central entries, and typical product labels. P = protein, C = carbohydrates, F = fat. Actual values vary by brand, can size, and the amount of oil, cheese, nuts, or dressing used. Nutrition source: USDA FoodData Central
