15-Day Metabolic Meal Plan

Structured in two phases to prevent spoilage in a 3.2 cu. ft. fridge while targeting blood sugar and lipids.

Phase 1: Days 1–7 (Fresh & Perishable Focus)

Day Breakfast Lunch Dinner
Day 1 Greek yogurt (½ cup), 1 tbsp chia, walnuts, ½ diced apple. Tuna & Chickpeas: ½ can chickpeas, 1 can tuna, cherry tomatoes, olive oil, pepper. Fiesta Bowl: ½ pouch brown rice, ½ can black beans, shredded rotisserie chicken, salsa, ¼ avocado.
Day 2 Savory oats (½ cup dry) microwaved in water; stir in fresh spinach, 2 tbsp black beans, hot sauce. Chicken Wrap: High-fiber wrap, rotisserie chicken, spinach, ¼ avocado, mustard. Salmon & Greens: Microwave steam-bag broccoli; serve with 1 can wild salmon, olive oil, lemon pepper.
Day 3 2 hard-boiled eggs mashed with ¼ avocado, salt, pepper; 1 whole apple on the side. Lentil Soup: Heat 1 can lentil soup, wilt in 1 cup spinach; top with 1 spoonful Greek yogurt. Warm Chicken & Veg: Warm shredded rotisserie chicken with remaining steamed broccoli, olive oil, and garlic powder.
Day 4 Greek yogurt (½ cup), 1 tbsp ground flaxseed, walnuts, cinnamon. Mediterranean Plate: ½ can rinsed chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, feta/cheddar cubes, 1 hard-boiled egg, olive oil. Sardine Bowl: 1 tin sardines in olive oil served over ½ pouch warm brown rice with salsa and spinach.
Day 5 Savory oats with 2 tbsp black beans, garlic powder, hot sauce, and 1 hard-boiled egg sliced on top. Chicken & Avo Wrap: Remaining rotisserie chicken, ¼ avocado, cherry tomatoes, and mustard in a high-fiber wrap. Microwave Chili shortcut: Heat 1 can black beans with salsa, cumin, and garlic; top with cheddar and Greek yogurt.
Day 6 Greek yogurt (½ cup), ½ diced apple, peanut butter (1 tbsp), chia seeds. Tuna Spinach Salad: 1 can tuna over remaining fresh baby spinach, halved cherry tomatoes, olive oil, and vinegar/lemon. Salmon & Rice: 1 can wild salmon over ½ pouch brown rice and steam-bag green beans.
Day 7 2 hard-boiled eggs with hot sauce; 1 sliced apple with 1 tbsp peanut butter. Pantry Hummus Wrap: Mash remaining chickpeas with olive oil and garlic in a wrap; add cheddar and hot sauce. Lentil Stew: Heat 1 can lentil soup; stir in any remaining fresh spinach and a dash of black pepper.

Phase 2: Days 8–15 (Shelf-Stable, Cabbage & Frozen Focus)

Note: Fresh spinach and rotisserie chicken are finished. Transition to hearty green cabbage (lasts 3+ weeks in mini-fridge), canned seafood/poultry, and steam-bag vegetables.

Day Breakfast Lunch Dinner
Day 8 Oatmeal (½ cup) with 1 tbsp peanut butter, cinnamon, and 1 tbsp ground flaxseed. Cabbage Slaw Wrap: Shredded green cabbage, 1 can light tuna, olive oil, mustard, wrapped in a tortilla. Black Bean & Rice Bowl: ½ can black beans, ½ pouch brown rice, salsa, topped with cheddar cheese.
Day 9 Oatmeal with chia seeds and 1 diced apple. Warm Lentil Plate: Heat 1 can lentil soup; stir in shredded raw cabbage during the last 30 seconds for crunch. Salmon & Frozen Broccoli: Microwave steam-bag broccoli; serve with 1 can wild salmon, olive oil, and pepper.
Day 10 Peanut butter oats: ½ cup rolled oats cooked with water; stir in 1 tbsp peanut butter and walnuts. Chickpea Salad: ½ can chickpeas tossed with olive oil, black pepper, and shredded cabbage. Pouched Chicken Bowl: 1 pouch chunk chicken breast warmed with ½ pouch brown rice, salsa, and black pepper.
Day 11 Oatmeal with ground flaxseed and cinnamon; 1 fresh apple on the side. Sardines & Tortilla: 1 tin sardines mashed with mustard and black pepper inside a high-fiber wrap with crunchy cabbage. Microwave Veggie Chili: 1 can black beans heated with salsa and garlic powder; serve over steamed green beans.
Day 12 Oatmeal with walnuts, chia seeds, and a dash of salt. Tuna & Chickpea Toss: 1 can tuna, ½ can chickpeas, olive oil, lemon juice or vinegar, black pepper. Pouched Chicken & Veg: Warm 1 pouch chicken breast; serve over microwave-steamed green beans with olive oil.
Day 13 1 sliced apple with 2 tbsp peanut butter; small bowl of plain oats with cinnamon. Cabbage & Lentil Bowl: 1 can lentil soup heated over a large bowl of finely sliced cabbage (wilts lightly). Salmon Fiesta: 1 can wild salmon mixed with salsa and ½ can rinsed black beans.
Day 14 Peanut butter & chia oats (½ cup oats, 1 tbsp PB, 1 tbsp chia). Chicken Wrap: 1 pouch chicken breast, shredded cabbage, cheddar cheese, mustard in a high-fiber wrap. Sardine Mediterranean Bowl: 1 tin sardines, ½ can chickpeas, olive oil, garlic powder, black pepper.
Day 15 Oatmeal with walnuts and 1 fresh apple. Pantry Clean-out Wrap: Remaining chickpeas and black beans mashed with salsa and cheddar inside a wrap. Tuna & Steamed Broccoli: 1 can tuna tossed with 1 bag steamed broccoli, olive oil, and hot sauce.
